# JobKaro

Your story, professionally told in English.

Write your profile in Roman Urdu, Urdu, Hindi or English — JobKaro turns it into a tailored professional English cover letter, a clean one-page resume, and 10 interview preparation questions for the job you paste in.

- Free forever — bring your own free Mercury (Inception Labs) API key; it stays only in your browser
- Interface in English, Urdu and Hindi
- Integrity first: it never invents experience, degrees or dates — it only polishes the facts you share
- No signup, nothing stored on any server

Live: https://sehrish64.github.io/jobkaro/
